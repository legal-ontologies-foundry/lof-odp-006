# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Versions follow semantic versioning
(LOF-P-006).

## [Unreleased]

- Generalized priority principle `ODP006_0001503` from *primacy of European
  Union law* to *primacy of an overriding legal order*, so that it covers
  supranational, federal, and treaty-based primacy in any legal regime
  (LOF-P-010). The EU-specific `dcterms:coverage` was removed; EU primacy,
  US federal preemption, Canadian paramountcy, Australian section 109, and UN
  Charter Article 103 are given as examples. The IRI is unchanged, since the
  pattern has not been accepted.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

- First draft for review by the LOF founding coordinators, revised after an
  internal review before publication (see the pattern page).
- Mappings to related standards published as SSSOM under `src/mappings/`.
